Agentic AI on Google Cloud · Live · Oct 2026

Waypoint

A multi-agent assistant that turns UTM's scattered degree rules, course data and timetable into cited, checked, term-by-term plans. Built end to end like a small forward-deployed engagement: messy sources, agents, evals, deployment.

  • Google ADK orchestrator and specialist agents; a self-critique agent raised valid plans from 2/6 to 4/6
  • Hybrid RAG on pgvector + full-text: 81% recall@5 and 67% accuracy on 21 held-out questions, at $0.011 per question
  • 3 MCP integrations, CI-gated evals, 9/9 live safety tests, OpenTelemetry cost and latency tracing

AI tooling · v2 shipped Oct 2026

ragaudit

A test kit for AI assistants that answer from documents. It writes its own quiz, grades every answer, tunes the assistant's settings, and keeps the score honest as the documents grow.

  • Generates test questions from your documents, reworded and checked by a second model
  • Re-tests only what changed when pages are added, edited or deleted
  • Tunes on practice questions, then scores once on a locked exam it never saw
  • 272 automated tests; real-model path tested locally with Ollama

Who's Real?

Quant tooling · Python + Rust

Agro-Quant

A signal engine for one agricultural stock, Adecoagro (AGRO). It reads the dollar, sugar prices and world news, then makes a logged buy, sell, hold or freeze call with the numbers behind it.

  • Rust core (PyO3) computes rolling stats; an ARIMA model forecasts the exchange rate
  • News risk acts as a veto before any price signal
  • Backtester replays two years of prices, rainfall and correlations

Python · Rust (PyO3) · statsmodels

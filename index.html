Agentic AI on Google Cloud · Live · V3 · Nov 2026

Waypoint

A U of T degree-planning assistant that answers from checked calendar data, maps prerequisite paths and critiques your term plan. Built like a small forward-deployed engagement: messy sources, agents, evals and deployment, then rebuilt from what testing it as a student uncovered.

  • Facts come from parsed calendar data and code, RAG only for policy text: 99.4% on a 167-question fact eval and 90% on 10 locked held-out policy questions
  • Streaming answers with live steps, a fast-path model router and an animated guide, Pip
  • Course map with prerequisite-path highlighting, a term planner with an automatic critic, a programs checklist and an enrolment check
  • Multi-agent Google ADK system, 3 MCP integrations, CI-gated evals with 9/9 safety tests, OpenTelemetry tracing

AI tooling · On PyPI · v0.4 · Nov 2026

ragaudit

A test kit for AI assistants that answer from documents. It writes its own quiz, grades every answer, tunes the assistant's settings on practice questions and scores it once on a locked exam. Now it's one install and a setup page in the browser.

  • First finding: an assistant scored 100% on answers but found the right page only 30.8% of the time; 9 of 13 passes were lucky guesses from model memory
  • Writes reworded test questions, checked by a second model and split by chunk into practice questions and a locked exam
  • One-command improve loop: on the demo docs with local models, the locked exam went from 3/5 to 5/5 (a small, 5-question exam)
  • Browser setup wizard, run history with fair before/after comparisons, 415 automated tests

Quant tooling · Python + Rust

Agro-Quant

A signal engine for one agricultural stock, Adecoagro (AGRO). It reads the dollar, sugar prices and world news, then makes a logged buy, sell, hold or freeze call with the numbers behind it.

  • Rust core (PyO3) computes rolling stats; an ARIMA model forecasts the exchange rate
  • News risk acts as a veto before any price signal
  • Backtester replays two years of prices, rainfall and correlations
